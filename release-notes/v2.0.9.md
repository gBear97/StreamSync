StreamSync 2.0.9 smooths out watch parties: viewers can join before anyone presses Play, and a party that ends tells you why.

## What's new

- **Viewers can join before anyone presses Play.** A host whose film hadn't played yet sent nothing, so viewers waited for the host's first Play. And a viewer whose own copy hadn't played yet stayed at 0:00 while the host was paused, because the player ignored the jump. Now viewers go straight to where the host is, paused on the host's frame.
- **A party that ends by itself says why in Watch party.** When a join is refused (the wrong film, say) or a party loses its relay, the reason used to show only in the main window's status line. Now Watch party shows it too: under **Join** for a viewer, under **Host** for a host.
- **A failed join keeps your session code.** Close Watch party and open it again, and the code is still there, with why the last session ended.
- **Hosting again after a party ended by itself works.** If a party had ended on its own and you pressed **Start hosting** without pressing **Leave** first, the button didn't turn into **Cancel** while the tunnel opened, and a tunnel left from the old party kept running, even after StreamSync quit. Now a new party first closes what's left of the last one.
- **Film position from stays as you set it.** The host's choice went back to "What this computer hears" each time Watch party opened. Now it's kept, and saved with your settings.
- **The timeline stays put when a message appears.** A status message that appeared, or ran onto a second line, pushed the timeline and the play button down, so a click aimed at them could miss. On Windows, "Couldn't find the stream window" did this just as you paused. The main window now keeps room for two lines of status, so nothing moves.
- **Mac updates leave nothing behind.** Each update on a Mac left a small script in your temp folder. 2.0.9 removes those when it starts, and its own updates delete theirs as they run. When it removes any, `streamsync.log` gets one line, such as "removed 6 old swap scripts".

## Updating

- **From 2.0.x:** accept the update offer in the app, or use **Settings > Check now**. Your settings are kept. On a Mac, the first start of 2.0.9 also clears away the scripts your earlier updates left.
- **From 1.x:** follow the "Upgrading from 1.x" steps in the [2.0.0 release notes](https://github.com/gBear97/StreamSync/releases/tag/v2.0.0). On Windows that means installing the setup program once by hand.
