StreamSync 2.0.2 makes watch parties work through a relay at a `wss://` address, so you can host one without renting a server.

## What's new

- **Watch party over wss:// on the Mac.** A relay at a `wss://` (encrypted) address, such as a Cloudflare tunnel, now works in the Mac app. Before, the Mac app couldn't verify the relay's certificate, and every attempt ended in "could not reach the relay".
- **Host a watch party with no server.** The host runs the relay on their own computer and makes it reachable with a free Cloudflare quick tunnel. There's no account, nothing to rent and no ports to open. The steps are in the README's [Relay server](https://github.com/gBear97/StreamSync#relay-server) section.
- **Paste the tunnel's address as it is.** When you press Host or Join, the **Relay server** field turns an `https://` address into `wss://`, and `http://` into `ws://`. Any other address that isn't a relay address is pointed out straight away, before anything starts.
- **Clearer relay errors.** When a party can't reach its relay, the message now says why, if the app can tell. For example, the address isn't ws:// or wss://, the certificate didn't verify, the relay behind the tunnel isn't running, or the tunnel has stopped.

## Updating

- **From 2.0.0 or 2.0.1:** accept the update offer in the app, or use **Settings > Check now**. Your settings are kept.
- **From 1.x:** follow the "Upgrading from 1.x" steps in the [2.0.0 release notes](https://github.com/gBear97/StreamSync/releases/tag/v2.0.0). On Windows that means installing the setup program once by hand.

On a Mac, 2.0.1 and earlier can't join a `wss://` relay. If your party uses one, ask Mac viewers to update first.
