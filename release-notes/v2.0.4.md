StreamSync 2.0.4 keeps the film window where you put it on Windows, and stops StreamSync from opening twice.

## What's new

- **Windows: the film window comes back where you left it.** With the built-in player, when the film pauses and your stream comes forward, StreamSync hides the film window, then shows it again when the film plays. A film window snapped to half the screen used to come back smaller, at its size from before the snap. Now it comes back the same size and in the same place, and maximized if it was maximized. A fullscreen film still comes back fullscreen, and leaving fullscreen afterwards puts it back where it was. A film window you closed or minimized stays that way. While the film window is up, **F** (or a double-click), **Esc** and **Open film…** no longer send it back to its pre-snap size either. One limit: a snapped window comes back where it was, but after that Windows treats it as an ordinary window, so dragging it no longer restores its pre-snap size.
- **One StreamSync at a time.** Opening StreamSync while it's already running no longer starts a second copy.
  - On Windows, the open copy comes to the front instead. If it shows no window within 5 seconds (it may still be starting), a message says StreamSync is already running and how to end it in Task Manager.
  - On the Mac, the Finder and the Dock already brought the open copy forward. Now `open -n` and running the app's program directly do too, if you've allowed StreamSync to control System Events (the permission "Show the stream while the film is paused" asks for). Without that permission, the second copy just closes.
  - While it runs, StreamSync keeps a small `.streamsync.lock` file in your home folder.
- **Clearer status after opening a film.** When you open a film in the built-in player, the status line now says "Loaded <film>. Sync to stream starts it where the stream is; Space plays it from here." It used to say "Playback starts on first sync.", though Space already played the film without a sync.

## Updating

- **From 2.0.x:** accept the update offer in the app, or use **Settings > Check now**. Your settings are kept.
- **From 1.x:** follow the "Upgrading from 1.x" steps in the [2.0.0 release notes](https://github.com/gBear97/StreamSync/releases/tag/v2.0.0). On Windows that means installing the setup program once by hand.
