StreamSync 2.0.6 hears the stream on a Mac by itself: no BlackHole, no audio routing.

## What's new

- **The Mac records its own sound.** On macOS 14.2 or later, StreamSync listens to what your Mac is playing through macOS's built-in audio tap: every app except StreamSync itself, so the film's own sound never counts. Nothing to install, no Multi-Output Device, and your volume keys keep working. The first time you sync, macOS asks whether StreamSync may record this computer's audio: allow it. If you said no, turn it on under **System Settings > Privacy & Security > Screen & System Audio Recording > System Audio Recording Only**.
- **Syncs line up with what you hear.** Each moment of sound arrives with the time macOS captured it, and StreamSync adds your speakers' or headphones' own delay, so the film matches what reaches your ears: a few milliseconds on built-in speakers, much more on Bluetooth headphones.
- **No more quiet fallback to the microphone.** Without BlackHole, StreamSync used to listen through the Mac's microphone without saying so. A microphone hears the whole room, including another computer playing the same stream seconds ahead, and adds its own delay, which put the film a couple of frames late. Now a microphone is used only when you pick one under **Settings > Listen to**, and when StreamSync hears nothing it says why: nothing is playing on this Mac, or macOS isn't letting it record yet.
- **BlackHole is optional.** It still works when picked under **Settings > Listen to**, and on macOS 14.0 and 14.1, which have no audio tap, StreamSync uses it when it's installed. The first-run check no longer offers to install it. If you set BlackHole up only for StreamSync, you can remove it and switch your sound output back to your speakers.

Nothing changes on Windows.

## Updating

- **From 2.0.x:** accept the update offer in the app, or use **Settings > Check now**. Your settings are kept. On a Mac, if **Settings > Listen to** names BlackHole or a microphone, pick **This Mac's sound** there instead.
- **From 1.x:** follow the "Upgrading from 1.x" steps in the [2.0.0 release notes](https://github.com/gBear97/StreamSync/releases/tag/v2.0.0). On Windows that means installing the setup program once by hand.
