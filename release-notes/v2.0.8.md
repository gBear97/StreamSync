StreamSync 2.0.8 lets you host a watch party with nothing to set up, and viewers now follow the host's pauses and jumps at once.

## What's new

- **Host a watch party with nothing to set up.** In **Watch party**, **Free Cloudflare tunnel** is ticked, and **Start hosting** does the rest. StreamSync runs the relay itself and opens a free Cloudflare tunnel to it, so there's no server to rent and nothing to run in a terminal. If Cloudflare's cloudflared isn't on your computer, StreamSync offers to download it into its own folder (about 20 MB on a Mac, 52 MB on Windows). It runs it only once Cloudflare's published checksum and signature check out. A free tunnel takes about 200 people at once, for as long as you host. To host through a relay server instead, untick the box.
- **Invite friends in one line.** **Copy invite** copies the address and the session code together. Friends paste the whole line into **Session code** and press **Join**.
- **Pauses and jumps reach viewers at once.** Viewers used to hear about a pause, a play or a jump only on the host's 2-second heartbeat, so they played on past a pause and then stepped back. Now each change is sent the moment it happens. A jump the host makes while paused now reaches paused viewers too.
- **The Watch party window no longer blocks the main window.** While it was open, the main window ignored every click, so a host's Play didn't work until it was closed. Now it opens beside the main window, and both work.
- **A viewer whose film won't play says so.** It used to say "Following the session" while standing still. Now it says why, and says when it's following again.
- **A full party says it's full.** When a free tunnel has no room left, a viewer is told the party is full, instead of "it answered HTTP 429, not as a relay".
- **Faster syncs on DTS-HD soundtracks.** Most Blu-ray remuxes carry DTS-HD audio. StreamSync now decodes just its core, which is about three times faster: a sync's search on such a film takes under a second instead of about two.

## Updating

- **From 2.0.x:** accept the update offer in the app, or use **Settings > Check now**. Your settings are kept.
- **From 1.x:** follow the "Upgrading from 1.x" steps in the [2.0.0 release notes](https://github.com/gBear97/StreamSync/releases/tag/v2.0.0). On Windows that means installing the setup program once by hand.
