StreamSync 2.0.4 keeps the film window where you put it on Windows, and stops StreamSync from opening twice.

## What's new

- **Windows: the film window comes back where you left it.** When the film pauses and your stream comes forward, StreamSync hides the film window and shows it again when the film plays. A film window snapped to half the screen or maximized used to come back smaller, at its older size. Now it comes back the same size and in the same place: maximized if it was maximized, and fullscreen if it was fullscreen. A film window you closed or minimized stays that way. **F**, **Esc** and **Open film...** no longer un-snap it either. One limit: a snapped window comes back where it was, but Windows treats it as an ordinary window after that, so dragging it no longer restores its pre-snap size.
- **One StreamSync at a time.** Opening StreamSync while it's already running now brings the open copy to the front instead of starting a second one. On the Mac, the Finder and the Dock already did this, and now every other way of opening the app does too. On Windows, if the open copy shows no window within 5 seconds, a message says StreamSync is running and how to close it in Task Manager.
- **Clearer status after opening a film.** The status line now says "Sync to stream starts it where the stream is; Space plays it from here." Before, it said playback would start on the first sync, though Space already played the film without one.

## Updating

- **From 2.0.x:** accept the update offer in the app, or use **Settings > Check now**. Your settings are kept.
- **From 1.x:** follow the "Upgrading from 1.x" steps in the [2.0.0 release notes](https://github.com/gBear97/StreamSync/releases/tag/v2.0.0). On Windows that means installing the setup program once by hand.
