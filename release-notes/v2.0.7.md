StreamSync 2.0.7 keeps a sync from failing when the film is slow to open.

## What's new

- **A film that's slow to open no longer fails the sync.** The first time StreamSync plays a film after it starts, macOS may ask whether StreamSync may open the film's folder (Downloads, Desktop, Documents or an external drive), and an external drive can take a moment to spin up. A sync used to give up after 6 seconds, and the film then started on its own from the beginning, out of sync. Now the sync waits while the film opens, for up to a minute, says what it's waiting for, and lands in the right place.
- **A missing film is reported straight away.** If the film has been moved or deleted, a sync says so in about a second ("is it still where it was?"), instead of after 6 seconds with "VLC did not start playback in time".
- **A film that has reached its end plays again.** Syncing, or pressing Play, after the end of the film used to do nothing. Now it starts the film again.

## Updating

- **From 2.0.x:** accept the update offer in the app, or use **Settings > Check now**. Your settings are kept.
- **From 1.x:** follow the "Upgrading from 1.x" steps in the [2.0.0 release notes](https://github.com/gBear97/StreamSync/releases/tag/v2.0.0). On Windows that means installing the setup program once by hand.
