StreamSync 2.0.5 cleans up after its own updates, and writes down what "show the stream while paused" did.

## What's new

- **Updates no longer leave their downloads behind.** Each update downloads into your temp folder, and on Windows its installer (about 44 MB) used to stay there for good, one per update. Now, each time StreamSync starts, it deletes the downloads earlier updates left behind. After an update, the new version also deletes the installer it was just installed from, once the installer has finished; it keeps trying for about a minute while Windows still holds the file. It only ever deletes folders named `streamsync-dl-…` directly in the temp folder, and never a download it has just started itself. When it deletes anything, `streamsync.log` gets one line, such as "removed 3 old update downloads (126 MB)".
- **The stream swap is now in the log.** With "Show the stream while the film is paused" on, `streamsync.log` now records each step of the swap and what started it: the pause or resume (Space, a play button, a sync, or auto mode), the film window being hidden and shown again and where, fullscreen being left and given back, and the stream window or app being brought forward or put away. Before, the swap wrote nothing, even when it failed, so a report like "my browser got minimized" couldn't be traced.

## Updating

- **From 2.0.x:** accept the update offer in the app, or use **Settings > Check now**. Your settings are kept. On Windows, the first start of 2.0.5 also clears away the installers from your earlier updates.
- **From 1.x:** follow the "Upgrading from 1.x" steps in the [2.0.0 release notes](https://github.com/gBear97/StreamSync/releases/tag/v2.0.0). On Windows that means installing the setup program once by hand.
